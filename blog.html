From a Tiny Idea to a Haunted Orchestra

What if Halloween monsters were musical instruments?

That simple idea became Monster Puppet Theater, an interactive musical toy featuring four customizable monsters performing inside an antique miniature theater.

Building with Kiro

I used Kiro Web's Spec-Driven Development workflow to organize the project into eight development tasks, supported by detailed requirements, architectural specifications, and persistent steering rules.

Instead of micromanaging every implementation detail, I gave Kiro musical constraints and artistic direction while allowing it to develop the artwork, animations, and technical solutions independently.

Each development milestone was delivered through a GitHub pull request.

An Unexpected Lesson in Digital Audio

During integration testing, Kiro discovered waveform discrepancies between successive audio loops.

At 44,100 Hz, our musical loop contains exactly 189,000 sample frames, which is not divisible by Web Audio's 128-frame render block.

Kiro investigated the problem and implemented a solution using an intermediate sample rate of 44,800 Hz and circular Kaiser-windowed sinc resampling.

Its numerical tests demonstrated substantially smoother loop boundaries.

Watching Kiro independently investigate and solve this problem was one of the most fascinating moments of the project.

Kiro Became a Video Producer

I also asked Kiro to create the demonstration video.

Despite the limitations of its cloud environment, Kiro developed its own recording pipeline using Playwright, timestamped screenshots, Web Audio capture, and Chromium's MediaRecorder.

It produced an English-language demonstration video featuring actual gameplay and synthesized music.

An Entire Project for 102 Credits

The entire development process, including implementation, procedural artwork, audio engineering, automated testing, and video production, consumed approximately 102 of my 4,000 Kiro credits.

What Kiro Changed for Me

As an independent game creator, I want to spend more time imagining experiences and less time struggling with implementation details.

Kiro's specification-driven workflow allowed me to concentrate on creative decisions while the agent handled much of the technical work.

What began as a small Halloween experiment became a complete interactive musical theater in a single day.

And there are still plenty of credits left for the next adventure!
